# Ridgeview Polytechnic Student Handbook (fictional)

Ridgeview Polytechnic is a fictional college made up for this codelab. None of these policies are real.

## Academic advising

Every full-time student has an assigned academic advisor. Advising appointments are 30 minutes and can be booked through the Campus Desk up to 14 days ahead. You can hold at most two upcoming advising appointments at a time. To cancel, give at least 24 hours' notice so the slot can go to another student.

## Course withdrawal deadlines

You can drop a course in the first two weeks of term (the add/drop period) with a full tuition refund and no record on your transcript. From week 3 to the end of week 9, you can withdraw from a course with a grade of W on your transcript and no refund. After week 9, you can't withdraw, except through a compassionate withdrawal approved by the Registrar.

## Compassionate withdrawal

If a serious illness, a family emergency or another situation outside your control stops you from finishing a course, you can apply to the Registrar for a compassionate withdrawal at any point in the term. You'll need supporting documents, such as a note from a health professional. If it's approved, you receive a W and may receive a partial refund, decided case by case.

## Academic integrity

Your work must be your own. Copying, submitting someone else's work, or using AI tools in ways your instructor hasn't allowed is an academic integrity violation. A first violation usually means a zero on the assignment and a meeting with the Academic Integrity Office. A second violation can lead to failing the course. Each course outline says which AI tools are allowed and how to cite them.

## Late assignments

Unless your course outline says otherwise, late assignments lose 10% per day, up to five days. After five days, late work receives a zero. If you have an approved accommodation, follow the timelines in your accommodation letter instead.

## Accessibility services

If you have a disability or a medical condition that affects your studies, Accessibility Services can arrange accommodations, such as extra time on tests. Register with Accessibility Services before your first test of the term if you can. Your diagnosis stays private; instructors only see the accommodations you need.

## Student privacy

The college keeps your student record private. Staff can only share your information with you, after confirming your identity, or with people you've given written permission. The Campus Desk will never share another student's information with you.
